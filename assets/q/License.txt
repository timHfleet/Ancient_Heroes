The OBJ files in this folder are a small subset of that pack
(common trees, pines, bushes, a mossy rock, and a flower clump).
No copyright is claimed. Attribution is appreciated and not required.

These meshes are recolored in the game with the project's toon materials.
The original MTL files are not included because they only store Blender
diffuse values and no textures.
